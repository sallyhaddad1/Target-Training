Our Vision

We believe that the training and development of UAE nationals is a fundamental pillar of sustainable growth and a core component of our corporate responsibility. In this context, the Target Engineering Construction – National Emirates Training (TEC-NET) initiative represents our structured commitment to nurturing national talent in alignment with the UAE’s workforce development priorities and long-term sustainability goals.

Through the TEC-NET program, we aim to invest in local capabilities, foster future leaders, and develop a diverse, skilled, and future-ready workforce that contributes to a resilient and prosperous economy. The initiative reflects our dedication to supporting the UAE’s strategic vision for sustainable economic growth, industrial excellence, and social development.

By empowering Emirati talent through TEC-NET, we reinforce our role as a responsible and forward-thinking organization, integrating sustainability, social impact, and workforce development into our operational framework while delivering long-term value for both the organization and the wider community.
