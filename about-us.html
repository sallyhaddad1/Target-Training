About Us

Target Engineering Construction Since 1975

TEC Company was established in 1975

The Company offers a single source EPC service with standalone specialties.

The Company has a Special Category classification in Buildings in Abu Dhabi and is licensed to build unlimited floors high rise towers in Abu Dhabi and Dubai.

With more than 45 years of experience, Target has established a strong customer base comprising of Oil & Gas Sector companies major EPC contractors, Government sector Clients and well known property developers.

Our Sepecialized Divisions

1. Mechanical Oil and Gas Division
2. Electrical Division
3. Civil Division (Industrial and Buildings)
4. Marine Division

Our Values

1. Commitment
We are committed to improving the quality of life and standard of living for people in the communities we develop. We do not rest on our accomplishments, but rather build on them to be the best we can.

2. Team spirit
We work together, across boundaries, to meet the needs of our customers, clients, shareholders and associates to help the company win. We create a “can do” spirit.

3. Integrity
We do the right thing in a reliable way, creating value both for our business and for our communities. We demonstrate honest, ethical behaviour in everything we do.

4. Professionalism
We demonstrate respect, moderation, objectivity, productivity and effectiveness in all that we do.

5. Accountability
We are personally responsible for delivering on our commitments as teams and as individuals.
